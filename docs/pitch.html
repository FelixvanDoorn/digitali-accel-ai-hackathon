From paper to data

Open with the people, not the tech. Field organisations exist to help people, but their staff lose hours to admin and their managers decide without data. Digitali takes the admin away and gives them the data.
The problem

Field organisations exist to help people. Too much of their time goes to admin.

Every day

Paper forms

Field staff record meetings, attendance, payments and visits on paper, in notebooks and in WhatsApp chats.

Every week

Retyped by hand

Staff copy it all into spreadsheets by hand. Hours that should go to the people they serve.

Every month

Decisions late

Without a database, managers decide on gut feeling. Weeks later, if the data arrives at all.

New apps and tablets fail on cost, training and adoption. So the paper stays, and the decisions stay blind.

Field heavy organisations: NGOs, cooperatives, microfinance, public services. The bottleneck is data input, not analytics.
The user

Less admin for the field officer. Better decisions for the manager.

  • How often: after every meeting or visit, several times a week per officer.
  • Today: paper to office, then manual entry, then a spreadsheet.
  • Would they pay: they already pay for data entry, in staff hours taken away from the field.

No more retyping

Data entry disappears.

Instant receipt

The officer gets a summary back: who paid, who is missing.

Fewer disputes

A timestamped record settles "I paid".

Same day numbers

Managers see results the day they happen.

The officer must gain something, or the photos stop in week three. The receipt and dispute record are the officer's reasons; same day numbers are the manager's.

Responsible design

The model proposes. A person confirms.

Privacy

No photos kept

Images are read once and discarded. Only confirmed fields are kept. All test data is synthetic.

Errors

Human in the loop

Nothing is saved until a person checks it. Unclear fields are flagged, every field is editable.

Misuse

Fixed task

The engine only fills a predefined record format. No free text generation, no decisions about people.

Umoja ni nguvu

Unity is strength.

Emphasise: the model never makes a decision about a person, it only transcribes, and a person confirms.
