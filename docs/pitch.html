The user

Meet Asha.

Field officer in western Kenya.
She supports 12 community groups.

Asha is a persona based on field officers at microfinance institutions, cooperatives and NGOs. She visits groups, runs meetings and records everything on paper.
The user

Every meeting ends on paper.

Attendance, savings, loans, visits.
Signed, stamped, filed.

The paper works in the field. No battery, no signal, everyone can sign. The problem starts after the meeting.
The problem

Her evenings go to retyping.

Hours of admin instead of hours with the people she serves.

Every sheet gets copied into a spreadsheet by hand. Slow, full of typos, and it takes her away from the field.
The problem

Her manager decides weeks later.

The data is there. It is just stuck on paper.

Head office sees repayment and attendance weeks late, if at all. Decisions run on gut feeling instead of data.
The solution

Asha takes a photo. That's it.

Digitali turns it into clean, structured data.
She checks it and moves on.

No new app, no new form, no training. She keeps the paper she already uses and sends a photo, today by web upload, next over WhatsApp.

Responsible by design

The model proposes. Asha confirms.

No photos stored. Unclear fields flagged.
Nothing saved without a person.

Text in the photo is treated as data, never as instructions. The output must match the template's schema. Photos are processed in memory and dropped; metadata such as GPS is stripped.
