The problem

Organisations want to decide on data. Their data is still on paper.

Every day

Paper forms

Meetings, attendance, payments and visits are recorded on sheets.

Every week

Retyped by hand

Office staff copy the sheets into a system. Slow, costly and error prone.

Every month

Decisions late

Management sees the numbers days or weeks later, if at all.

How it works

No new app. Staff only take a photo.

1 Input

Photo

Any form, sheet or log, sent through a channel staff already use.

2 Engine

Vision model

An open model on Nebius Token Factory reads the photo and matches rows to your reference list.

3 Output

Structured data

Validated records, reviewed by a person, then exported or sent by API.
