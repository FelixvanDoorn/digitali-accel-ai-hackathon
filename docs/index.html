Open with the problem, not the tech. One sentence: field organisations run on paper and decide blind; Digitali turns the paper they already use into data.
The problem

Field organisations want to decide on data. Their data is still on paper.

Every day

Paper forms

Field staff record meetings, attendance, payments and visits on sheets they have used for years.

Every week

Retyped by hand

Office staff copy the sheets into a system or spreadsheet. Slow, costly, full of typos.

Every month

Decisions late

Management sees the numbers days or weeks later, if the data arrives at all.

New apps and tablets fail on cost, rollout and frontline adoption. So the paper stays.

Field heavy organisations: NGOs, cooperatives, lenders with many branches. The bottleneck is data input, not analytics.
The user

The field officer who fills in the sheet, and the manager who needs it.

  • How often: after every meeting or visit, several times a week per officer.
  • Today: paper to office, then manual entry, then a spreadsheet.
  • Would they pay: the organisation pays for data entry already, in staff hours.

No more retyping

Data entry disappears.

Instant receipt

Officer gets a summary back: who paid, who is missing.

Fewer disputes

A timestamped record settles "I paid".

Same day numbers

Managers see targets the day they happen.

The officer must gain something, or the photos stop in week three. The receipt and dispute record are the officer's reasons; same day numbers are the manager's.

Responsible design

The model proposes. A person confirms.

Privacy

No photos kept

Images are processed and discarded. Only the confirmed fields are stored. Test data is synthetic.

Errors

Human in the loop

Nothing is saved until a person reviews it. Low confidence fields are flagged, every field is editable.

Misuse

Fixed task

The engine only fills a predefined template. No free text generation, no decisions about people.

Emphasise: the model never makes a decision about a person, it only transcribes.
